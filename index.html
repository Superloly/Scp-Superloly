  • Black and white hair but mostly black
  • Flying books with gems/crystals next to it
  • SCP Foundation (O5) clothes
  • Weird wings on the back

  • Black eye corruption with a purple horn

Personality

  • A mean person who doesn't care about others
  • Doesn't get mad quickly
  • Pessimist
  • Psychopath (mostly shown happy)
  • Crazy determined to get his crystals back
  • Calm

Powers / Anomalies

SCP-Superloly uses powers from his books, although the true source of power are the crystals connected to them. When SCP-Superloly places a crystal inside its matching book, the book becomes active like a powered device. Foundation personnel compare the books to “receivers” and the crystals to “batteries”.


  • Crystal of Mischieving / Chaos (Apollyon Scenario) — Gains the ability to manipulate people's minds.
  • Crystal of Teleportation (Apollyon Scenario) — Gains the ability to teleport.
  • Crystal of Protection (Keter) — Makes SCP-Superloly immune to pain and resistant against Foundation suppression radiowaves.
  • Crystal of Wisdom (Euclid) — Allows SCP-Superloly to know nearly everything, including classified personnel information.
  • Crystal of Strength (Euclid-Keter) — Grants unstoppable physical strength without visible muscle increase.
  • Crystal of Elements (Keter) — Able to generate almost every known Earth element. Dangerous radioactive materials appear unavailable.
  • Whenever SCP-Superloly obtains a book, Foundation personnel are ordered to immediately separate the crystal from it before activation becomes possible.

FOUNDATION LORE FILE

In this section you will get to know SCP-Superloly, one of the only SCPs without a numerical classification. Superloly used to be an MTF worker at the SCP Foundation and was considered one of the best operatives the Foundation had ever seen. After four years of service he was promoted to Site Director in 1992 and assigned to Site-17. After a major breach involving SCP-053, Site Director Superloly personally handled containment after security guards failed to stop the anomaly. He succeeded, and the entire site viewed him as a hero. Not long afterward O5-3 died from unknown causes. The O5 Council required a replacement and eventually selected Superloly. Unlike most O5 members who remain hidden, Superloly frequently visited Foundation sites worldwide to ensure personnel morale and operational stability. One day Superloly visited Site-19. As he approached the exit alarms suddenly activated and every door sealed shut. SCP-049 had breached containment. While returning to investigate, SCP-049 appeared directly before him holding a strange crystal that did not appear to originate from Earth. “I can cure you, my friend.” SCP-049 explained he had carried the crystal for a very long time. Superloly calmly told SCP-049 he was not sick and politely ordered him to stand down. As expected, SCP-049 touched him, killing him instantly before beginning a surgical operation. At the end of the procedure SCP-049 used the crystal. SCP-Superloly was born. “What did you do to me!?” Moments later SCP-Superloly used an unknown telekinetic force to attack SCP-049. The Doctor managed to remove the crystal from nearby floating books before Foundation personnel arrived. Personnel asked O5-Superloly if he was alright. Instead, he attacked the responding staff with violent telekinesis while SCP-049 was successfully recontained. The crystals later scattered worldwide after SCP-049 triggered a pre-planned failsafe. SCP-Superloly was eventually contained within Site-19 and officially designated as an SCP entity. Due to the immense respect personnel once had for O5-Superloly, he was never assigned a number. Instead he retained his former identity as SCP-Superloly. The first truly good man became evil.
